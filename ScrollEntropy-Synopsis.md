ScrollEntropy is a sovereign cryptographic protocol that transforms authored identity, intention, and structure into quantum-resilient capsules. These capsules are:

- 📜 Immutable scrolls sealed with GPG and AES256  
- 🧬 NFT-compatible payloads with authorship metadata  
- 📡 IPFS-pinned bundles with deterministic CIDs  
- 🧾 DAO-ready proposals and audit primitives  
- 🧠 Symbolic interfaces for decentralized cognition

We’ve moved from white paper to operational proof. Every scroll is now:

- Testable  
- Reproducible  
- Auditable  
- Renderable in-browser  
- Verifiable via sigil and entropy lineage
