# 🧑‍🏫 ScrollChain Curriculum for Isenberg School of Management

## Overview
This curriculum introduces students to symbolic governance, cryptographic authorship, and decentralized publishing using ScrollChain.

## Modules

### I. Scrolls as Sovereign Objects
- What is a scroll?
- Symbolic authorship and intent encoding

### II. Cryptographic Sealing
- GPG, AES256, and entropy bundling
- ScrollSigil.Toroid format

### III. IPFS & Provenance
- Content-addressable storage
- Pinning and manifest creation

### IV. DAO Governance
- Scrolls as proposals, votes, and audit trails
- DAO verticals: VaultPolis, ShiftHaven, ScholarDAO

### V. NFT Payloads
- Metadata, traits, and authorship capsules

### VI. Scroll Viewers
- Sovereign dashboards
- Visual pin explorers

## Capstone Project
Students will fork the ScrollChain repo, publish a scroll, and propose a DAO initiative.
